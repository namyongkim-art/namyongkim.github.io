KIM NAMYONG — DIFFERENCE

WORKS
ABOUT

KIM NAMYONG explores how we perceive and understand space through the relationship between actual materiality and pictorial representation.

Working directly on real wood, he uses light and shadow, perspective, and pictorial illusion to construct spaces and depths that do not physically exist. As wood, a tangible material, and painting, an image, overlap within a single plane, the boundary between reality and representation becomes unstable.

Beginning with Actuality and Reality (實際와 實在), his practice has expanded from the representation of objects toward the fragmentation and reconstruction of space. His recent work focuses on constructing multiple depths within a single pictorial plane.

By constructing unreal space upon physically existing material, Kim investigates the structure and instability of perception.
